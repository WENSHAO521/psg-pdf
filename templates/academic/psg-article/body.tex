\section{Introduction}
This sample article shows the PSG Research Article template. It is typeset with pdf\LaTeX{},
and its references are processed by Biber through Bib\LaTeX{}~\cite{psg-sample-handbook,psg-sample-article}.
The title block, running headers, licence line and DOI link all come from the publication
metadata that PSG Academic PDF writes to \texttt{psg-metadata.tex}.

\section{Methods}
Inline mathematics such as $E = mc^2$ and displayed equations are supported:
\begin{equation}
  \int_0^1 x^n \, \mathrm{d}x = \frac{1}{n+1}, \qquad n \geq 0.
  \label{eq:integral}
\end{equation}
Equation~\eqref{eq:integral} is numbered automatically.
